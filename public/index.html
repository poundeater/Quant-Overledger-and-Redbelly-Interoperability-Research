Could Redbelly connect to Quant's Overledger?

A feasibility report for the Redbelly DAO community (TASK-29). Sources opened and read on 19, 20 and 21 October 2026.

Read the reportSourcesEvidence

The short answers

Could Overledger's Gateway reach Redbelly (read data, send signed transactions)?

Not shown so far. Redbelly has a public EVM RPC, and an Ankr endpoint for it, and Ankr is an approved Overledger provider. But Redbelly was not in Overledger's live network list, and our test registration of the Ankr endpoint was refused as an unsupported network.

Analysis

Can Redbelly join the Fusion Rollup (deposits, unified balances, cross-chain messages)?

Not listed today, as far as public sources show.

Analysis

Do Receptor credentials and Tokeniser rules travel with an asset to another chain?

No, not by themselves.

Analysis

Is there an announced agreement between Redbelly and Quant?

None found.
