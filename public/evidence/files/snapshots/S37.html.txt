            <div>
              <p class="sp-kicker">The problem today</p>
              <h2 class="sp-title">Lock-up periods that deter investors</h2>
              <p class="sp-body">
                In most private funds, investors can exit only at scheduled
                redemption windows, and meeting redemptions may require the sale
                of assets. For many investors, this lock-up is a key reason not
                to invest.
              </p>

      <section class="sp-changes" aria-label="What changes for your fund">
        <div class="sp-inner">
          <h2 class="sp-title">What changes for your fund</h2>
          <div class="sp-changes__grid">
            <article class="sp-change">
              <h3 class="sp-change__title">Capital stays in the fund.</h3>
              <p class="sp-change__text">
                Unlike a redemption, a secondary transfer does not draw on fund
                capital. The position moves to a new holder, and your investment
                strategy is preserved.
              </p>
            </article>
            <article class="sp-change">
              <h3 class="sp-change__title">Fewer barriers to investment.</h3>
              <p class="sp-change__text">
                Investors commit more readily when an exit path is available.
                This addresses a common objection to private funds and supports
                AUM growth.
              </p>
            </article>
            <article class="sp-change">
              <h3 class="sp-change__title">A structural advantage.</h3>
              <p class="sp-change__text">
                Liquidity between redemption windows is uncommon in private
                markets. Offering secondary transfers differentiates your fund
                from traditional operators.
              </p>
            </article>
          </div>
        </div>
      </section>
